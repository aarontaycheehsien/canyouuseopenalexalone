# OpenAlex evidence synthesis experiments

The reports on this branch use the Stansfield diabetic eye screening benchmark. The [revised Substack draft](stansfield_pilot/substack/openalex-evidence-synthesis.md) explains how the searches changed and what the benchmark establishes.

| Report | Evidence |
|---|---|
| [2 November 2026 benchmark audit](stansfield_pilot/audit_2026-11-02/README.md) | Eight strategies, complete OQL, corrected study IDs, per-study retrieval, archived API responses and an offline verifier. The exploratory `unscreen*` variant recovered 130/130 indexed studies in 3,892 results. |
| [12 November 2026 independent-review transfer test](transfer_benchmark_2026-11-12/README.md) | Queries frozen before inspecting the insomnia review's included titles. All three retrieved 27/29 original articles (93.1%), below the prespecified >95% target; complete exports, diagnostics and an offline verifier are retained. |
| [12 November 2026 larger-workload transfer test](large_transfer_benchmark_2026-11-12/README.md) | Depression-biomarker review reporting 67,464 screening records (not a verified unique count). Frozen queries recovered 66/75 and 63/75; unrestricted counts, benchmark membership, partial-export limitations and diagnostics are retained. |
| [12 November 2026 transgenerational-inheritance test (branch `opus5.5`)](walker_transgenerational_benchmark_2026-11-12/README.md) | Walker et al. (2018): 63,754 records screened, single text-word PubMed strategy. Frozen OQL POST queries recovered 249/257 references (96.9%), meeting the >95% target and matching the published PubMed strategy re-run today, but returned 306,031 results (4.8× the screening load). Restricted-membership evidence, diagnostics and an offline verifier are retained. |
| [13 November 2026 multi-source inclusion test (branch `opus5.5`)](inclusion_sen_benchmark_2026-11-13/README.md) | Dalgaard et al. (2022), Campbell: nine databases plus grey literature, 20,183 records screened, 94 studies. Only 79/94 studies exist in OpenAlex (most absentees are US dissertations). Frozen OQL POST translations recovered 22–61/94 (best 64.9% at 151,147 results); the loss is driven by subject-heading lines with no OpenAlex equivalent and missing abstracts. |
| [Bt maize multi-source test (branch `opus5.5`)](bt_maize_multisource_benchmark_2026-11-12/README.md) | Meissle et al. (2022): 12 databases plus specialist sites, 12,967 records screened, 166 included references. 138/166 exist in OpenAlex; both frozen OQL POST arms recovered 121/166 (72.9%) with 6,775–7,053 results. Complete exports, facet diagnostics and an offline verifier are retained. |
| [Cost-of-mental-disorders multi-source test (branch `opus5.5`)](cost_mental_disorders_benchmark_2026-11-13/README.md) | Christensen et al. (2020): six databases plus bibliography searching, 13,579 records screened, 143 included articles. 141/143 exist in OpenAlex. Frozen OQL POST translations of the published PubMed strings recovered 104/143 (title-abstract, 33,520 results) and 116/143 (with keywords, 40,605 results); the same string re-run in PubMed today finds 86/143. |
| [Motor-competence multi-source test (branch `opus5.5`)](motor_competence_benchmark_2026-11-13/README.md) | Bao et al. (2024): six databases plus reference lists, 12,117 records screened, 44 included studies, all in OpenAlex. Both frozen OQL POST arms recovered 43/44 (97.7%) with 12,012 and 7,807 results, meeting both the recall and workload criteria; complete exports retained. |
| [Precarious-employment multi-source test (branch `opus5.5`)](precarious_employment_benchmark_2026-11-13/README.md) | Pulford et al. (2022): nine health and social-science databases, 12,940 records screened, 50 included studies; 48/50 in OpenAlex. The published Scopus string recovered 38/50 at 8,140 results; its employment block alone (as used in the health databases) recovered 44/50 at 42,085. |
| [HIV care-continuum multi-source test (branch `opus5.5`)](hiv_care_continuum_benchmark_2026-11-13/README.md) | Haw et al. (2025): PubMed, Embase and African Index Medicus plus hand-search, 10,281 records screened, 190 included articles, all in OpenAlex. The frozen Embase free-text translation recovered 134/190 (7,750 results) on title-abstract and 135/190 (7,897) with keywords; the age concept, which the review resolved partly through subject headings, accounts for most misses. Complete exports retained. |
| [Physical-activity primary-care multi-source test (branch `opus5.5`)](pa_primary_care_benchmark_2026-11-13/README.md) | Kettle et al. (2022): 12 databases and 3 trial registers, 14,566 records screened, 51 included studies; 48/51 in OpenAlex. The frozen MEDLINE free-text translation recovered 39/51 studies at 4,414 results on title-abstract and 40/51 at 4,543 with keywords; misses are spread across the primary-care, RCT-filter and physical-activity blocks. Complete exports retained. |
| [Anxiety and eating-disorder designed-search test (branch `opus5.5`)](anxiety_eating_benchmark_2026-11-13/README.md) | Trompeter et al. (2025): 6 databases, 19,591 screened, 54 studies, all in OpenAlex. Mechanical translation 42/54 at 21,566; designed search (text OR OpenAlex keywords per concept) 48/54 at 20,271, and 48/54 at 9,424 with a prospective-design concept. |
| [Ethnicity and diabetes-medication adherence designed-search test (branch `opus5.5`)](ethnicity_adherence_benchmark_2026-11-13/README.md) | Asiri et al. (2023): 4 databases plus grey literature, 17,410 screened, 41 studies, all in OpenAlex. Mechanical translation 34/41 at 9,320; designed search 36/41 at 6,908, and 36/41 at 3,418 with medication-context proximity. |
| [Smoke-free policy support designed-search test (branch `opus5.5`)](smokefree_support_benchmark_2026-11-13/README.md) | Boderie et al. (2023): 6 databases, 6,229 screened, 107 reports, 103 in OpenAlex. Mechanical 93/107 at 7,307; designed 92/107 at 10,282 and 89/107 at 4,013. The source strategy was already proximity-based. |
| [VR stroke rehabilitation designed-search test (branch `opus5.5`)](vr_stroke_benchmark_2026-11-13/README.md) | Soleimani et al. (2024): 5 databases, 7,381 screened, 55 RCTs, 54 in OpenAlex. Mechanical 22/55 at 18,063; designed 35/55 at 1,536. 18 misses are robotic or BCI trials the review classes as VR. |
| [NLP cognitive-impairment designed-search test (branch `opus5.5`)](nlp_cognitive_benchmark_2026-11-13/README.md) | Shankar et al. (2025): 8 databases, 5,033 screened, 51 studies, all in OpenAlex. Mechanical 25/51 at 1,027; designed 46/51 at 4,725 and 45/51 at 4,033. |
| [Not indexed vs missed by the search, and failure classification (branch `opus5.5`)](RECALL_SPLIT.md) | All 15 benchmarks, with each review classified against recall > 95% and records ≤ the authors' screening load: 4 coverage-impossible (independent of search strategy); 1 pass, 1 load failure, 3 coverage-no-margin and 6 search failures, all verdicts on the strategies as tested (translated published strategies or the author's designed searches). |
| [Health workers' mental health, EMRO designed-search test (branch `opus5.5`)](hcw_mental_emro_benchmark_2026-11-13/README.md) | Salahi Ardekani et al. (2025): 3 databases, 11,572 screened, 89 studies, 85 in OpenAlex. Mechanical 77/89 at 15,916; designed with the review's region added 82/89 at 7,176. |
| [Call-handlers' mental health designed-search test (branch `opus5.5`)](call_handlers_benchmark_2026-11-13/README.md) | Osório et al. (2024): 10 databases plus hand-search, 11,571 screened, 37 articles, 27 in OpenAlex (six absent from one practitioner journal). Mechanical 17/37 at 4,677; designed 24/37 at 9,179. |
| [3 November follow-up checks](stansfield_pilot/followup_2026-11-03/README.md) | Live re-run of the final query (3,892 results, 130/130) and a version using OpenAlex's adjN → within N−1 rule (3,857 results, 130/130). |
| [1 November G2 fact-check](stansfield_pilot/queries/G2_CORRECTED.md) | Correct placement of the topic filter and the effects of using tighter proximity distances. |
| [1 November Embase Table 8 translation](stansfield_pilot/queries/embase_table8_oql_2026/README.md) | A separate translation of the published Embase strategy and its benchmark results. |
| [October pilot](stansfield_pilot/PILOT_REPORT.md) | Original search arms, source retrieval and known-study membership. |
| [Cursor-order study](stansfield_pilot/cursor_study_2026-10-04/CURSOR_ORDER_REPORT.md) | Ranking and pagination evidence. |
